## Precedence — read this before citing anything

Sources in this packet disagree, because they were written at different times. When two sources conflict, the later one wins:

1. **`02-cpt-update-memo-2026-09-08.md`** (Sept 8, 2026) is the most recent policy statement. It **supersedes the handbook** on internships: summer experiential learning becomes a **required core component of the MBA beginning with the Class of 2028**, pending approval by MIT's Committee on Graduate Programs.
2. **`03-recruiting-guidelines-2026-2027.md`** supersedes the handbook's pointer to the AY2025–26 recruiting guidelines.
3. **`01-mba-policy-handbook-2025-2026.md`** is the AY**2025–26** edition, addressed to the Classes of **2026 and 2027**. It is authoritative for standing policy and process, but its dates are for the wrong academic year and it does not formally cover the Class of 2028. No 2026–27 edition had been published as of 2026-09-13.

**Rule:** for dates, use the orientation decks and the recruiting guidelines. For the internship requirement, use the CPT memo. For everything else, the handbook — while noting the edition gap.
